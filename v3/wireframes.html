    Share a screenshot from any app; it opens in the editor (S35).

    Keep you sharp, blur the street and strangers (S14).

From FRME

  • Black + #FE2C55 pink, library as Home, numbered picks, “N selected →”.
  • Editor: ✕ · undo · redo · Export; icon tiles; panels with ✕ · name · ✓.
  • Draw (path), Stickers, Ratio → Crop, export carousel + share circles, Projects, paywall.

Decisions for you

  • Video on free: pins + follow with finger, and face finding flags uncovered moments (never blocks export) — right balance?
  • Batch is Pro after the first project (from your “this is pro” note) — the right tile?
  • 4K is Pro (from your “this is paid” note on the export sheet) and the Projects screen is gone (projects live on Home) — both right?
  • 10 s rule: free users export the first 10 seconds of a video. Full length is Pro. Is that what you meant?
  • Pro = Track object + Batch + Live Cam + 4K + full-length video + no watermark + emoji + stickers. Keep $3.99 / $29.99 / $44.99?
  • Watermark: small “Blurit” mark, bottom-right, on free exports only (none on the first project) — size and place OK?
  • Emoji in Live Cam: follows the same rule (Pro after the first project) — OK?
  • Should we add more sticker packs over time so Pro keeps growing?
  • FRME tools left out: Filters, Text, Text brush, Cutout, Backgrounds. Bring any back?
  • Font: Manrope (FRME uses Google Sans) — keep?

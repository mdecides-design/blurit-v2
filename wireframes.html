Blurit V2 — cover anyone, any way.

Built on the current covers flow (VEIL prototype), pushed further: smart scan with people grouping, six cover families (blur types, emoji & flowers, stickers, shapes, AI), gap-free tracking, audio privacy and a privacy check before every share. 29 screens across 6 stages (8 new states in revision 2). Grey = structure, periwinkle = primary action, numbered pins = notes.

What’s new in V2

NewSmart Scan + People

Finds faces, people, screens, text and plates on-device; groups the same person across the whole video.

NewEveryone except me

One rule to hide everyone but you — remembers “This is me”.

NewCover Studio

6 families: Blur, Pixel, Emoji, Stickers, Shape, AI — each badged Privacy-grade or Stylistic (Kloak). Emoji can match expressions.

NewFlower & sticker covers

Soft, playful covers (🌸🌼) plus sticker packs and stickers cut from your own photos.

NewCover lanes + gap fixer

Each cover gets a lane on the timeline; red gaps show uncovered frames, one-tap fix.

NewTap to cover anything

Tattoos, house numbers, logos: tap, brush or lasso — and track it.

NewAudio privacy

Bleep spoken names and addresses; optional voice disguise.

NewPrivacy Check

A pre-share score with one fix per warning, including location metadata.

NewRecipes & batch

Save your choices and apply them to 50 photos.

Wild ideaLive Cam

Record with covers already on — nothing uncovered is ever saved.
